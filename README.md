# HR Analytics Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## Project Overview
An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Excel to analyze employee data and identify key workforce trends. This project focuses on understanding employee demographics, department-wise distribution, salary patterns, and attrition insights through clear and meaningful visualizations.

## Business Objectives
- Understand overall workforce distribution across departments.
- Analyze employee attrition across different departments and job roles.
- Explore employee demographics based on age and gender.
- Examine the relationship between overtime and employee attrition.
- Compare average monthly income across departments.
- Analyze workforce distribution by business travel category.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualization

## Dashboard Highlights
- KPI cards displaying key HR metrics.
- Department-wise employee distribution.
- Employee attrition analysis by department and job role.
- Gender-wise and age-group employee distribution.
- Average monthly income by department.
- Overtime vs. employee attrition analysis.
- Employee distribution by business travel.

## Dataset

- **Dataset Name:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance
- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Dataset Type:** Employee HR Data
- **Records:** 1,470 employee records

The dataset contains employee-related information such as age, gender, department, job role, monthly income, job satisfaction, overtime, and attrition status.

It was used to analyze workforce distribution and identify employee attrition patterns.
